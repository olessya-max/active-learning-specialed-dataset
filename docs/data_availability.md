## Data Availability Statement

This research dataset is available from the corresponding authors upon reasonable request.

Public access to the full dataset is restricted in order to protect participant privacy and to comply with institutional ethical requirements. The repository contains de-identified, aggregated, and analysis-ready data sufficient to verify the results reported in the associated publication.

Full raw reflective diary texts and any information that could potentially enable participant identification are not publicly shared. De-identified subsets and additional documentation may be provided to qualified researchers upon request.
